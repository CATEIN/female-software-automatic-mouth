Which voice is she?

You will hear ten short sentences, each spoken by two computer voices, A and B. Both come from the same 1982 speech synthesizer and speak at the same average pitch. Which one sounds more like a woman?

For each pair, play both voices (as often as you like), then answer two questions: which sounds more like a woman, and which is clearer. The order is shuffled for every listener. Your score and what everyone else chose appear at the end.
